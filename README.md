# AgriScore — Public Website

Marketing and information site for **AgriScore**, an AI-powered credit scoring
platform for smallholder farmers in Zimbabwe.

> "Giving Zimbabwe's farmers a financial identity — no collateral, no bank account required."

## The problem

- **$750M–$1.5B** annual agricultural credit gap in Zimbabwe
- **92%** of smallholder farmers lack access to formal credit
- **1.5 million** farmers excluded from financial systems due to absence
  of traditional credit history or collateral

AgriScore addresses this by scoring farmers using alternative data signals,
enabling lenders to extend credit to previously invisible borrowers.

## Stack

Pure HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks or build tools —
intentionally lightweight for fast load times and easy hosting.

## Status

AgriScore is currently in the ideation and pre-deployment stage. This site
represents the product vision and is actively updated as the platform develops.
